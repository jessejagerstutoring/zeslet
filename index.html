How to play

Guess the five-letter word in six tries. There is a new word every day, the same for everyone.

  • Each guess must be a real five-letter word.
  • The colours show how close you are.
BREAD

B is in the right spot.

STONE

O is in the word, but in a different spot.

LIGHT

G is not in the word.

The ghost

You are not playing alone. After each of your guesses, the ghost guesses too. Above the board you see only its colours, never its letters. Solve the word in fewer tries and you beat the ghost. At the end you see which words it tried.

Everyone faces the same ghost on the same day. You can turn it off in the settings.
